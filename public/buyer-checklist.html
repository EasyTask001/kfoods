Use this checklist to prepare a rice, lentil and pulse assortment discussion for a supermarket, club store, distributor or foodservice business in Canada or the United States.

Download product range (CSV)Prepare an inquiry
A discussion checklist — not a supplier qualification certificate. Listed package sizes come from the website catalogue. Pricing, live availability, minimum order quantities, certifications, regulatory compliance, capacity and retailer acceptance must be confirmed with K Foods. No affiliation or listing with Costco, Walmart, Loblaws or Real Canadian Superstore is implied.

01 / Your assortment

  • Product names, varieties and proposed pack sizes
  • Target category: rice, lentils, beans or other pulses
  • Retail format, consumer need and proposed shelf placement
  • Sample requirements and product evaluation process

02 / Commercial details

  • Estimated opening order and ongoing volume
  • Quote currency, validity, payment and minimum order terms
  • Case pack, pallet pattern, dimensions and weight
  • Launch timing and replenishment expectations

03 / Product documentation

  • SKU-specific ingredients, allergens and nutrition information
  • Product specifications, shelf life and storage conditions
  • Country of origin, lot traceability and recall contacts
  • GTIN/UPC and case identifiers; verify with the brand owner
  • Applicable food-safety audits and current certificates, if required

04 / Market & logistics

  • Delivery address / distribution centre, freight and lead time
  • Importer of record and responsibilities, if applicable
  • Canadian labelling, language and CFIA requirements
  • US labelling, import and FDA requirements, as applicable
  • Retailer-specific supplier onboarding and insurance requirements
